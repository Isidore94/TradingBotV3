# Desk rules by area

Only read the section for the area you're touching. Each rule is binding. The incident
behind each one is in `notes/docs/DESK_INTERNALS.md` (local only); open the matching
entry there only if you plan to change the rule. If you change a rule, update this line
in the same commit.

## Shape and data
- One desk role. Desk Link/satellite, the mini-PC scanner, Tk UI, `TickerMover.py` and PyQt5 are REMOVED.
- Market data: IBKR `127.0.0.1:7496` primary, yfinance fallback, source tracked per scan. D1 daily bars are pinned to Yahoo by `local_settings.json` `daily_bars_source: "yahoo"` (that's intended, not an IB failure).
- In-process `yf.download` goes through `yahoo_download.download` - yfinance's shared result dict is not thread-safe, and two overlapping downloads hung the Movers, Strength, Industry and Warehouse threads forever (2026-09-24). A guard test lists any direct caller; only the ask-first `master_avwap_lib/legacy.py` (scan_worker subprocess) is exempt.
- `longs.txt`/`shorts.txt` are day-trade lists, wiped whole after each session close by `scripts/daytrade_watchlist_reset.py` (decision 0020). It runs on the first Auto Pilot tick in every mode, before weekend/open-scan exits; names typed after the close survive to the next close. Each removed name gets one `session_reset` row. Swing lists are never touched.
- `C:\TradingBotData` is a plain local folder, not a cloud drive. Machine caches live under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TradingBotV3`. The DAS is the durable tier: write locally first, move to the DAS after.
- Shadow engines (`market_state.py`, `greatness_monitor`) only emit JSONL evidence.

## Research warehouse (shadow only, zero detector/score/alert influence)

- The post-scan build runs in a below-normal CHILD PROCESS, never a thread. Reads are month-keyed through `ResearchStore.read_rows`.

A reconstructed anchor is labelled and never counts as promotion evidence; repair order is build, rebuild daily features, recompute outcomes, then check band coverage.

- `bar_d1` always carries `BENCHMARK_SYMBOLS` (SPY/QQQ/IWM), members or not. A `setup_market_context` fix is a new `bias_definition_id`, never a rewrite; old rows stay and readers take the newest definition per occurrence/timeframe (`newest_context_rows`).

## Alert Center and review
- The arm bar stays UNDER the chart. Never propose moving it without asking. Rail shortcuts are bound at panel scope; hidden-tab shortcuts and duplicate bindings do not work.
- A VETO retires the chart. A CLAIMED like advances it. A QUICK like (Alt+L) and a NOTE move nothing. Both likes write `like_advance`.
- A claimed like on a D1 chart saves to `claimed_picks.jsonl` before retiring. A failed save keeps the chart open; a successful claim does not `remove_today`, park, or drop Focus. An active claim hides that symbol/side D1 scan alert from the REVIEW QUEUE only; M5 alerts, chart watches and evidence writers are untouched. Use `claimed_picks.claim_horizon`, never the rail timeframe.
- Only a successfully saved, identity-matching veto may arm a follow-up, and only through `AlertCenterPanel.VETO_FOLLOW_UPS`: `too_extended_from_base` -> M30/H1 Pullback, `incoming_trendline` -> trendline break + retest on the nearest incoming scan line (frozen at arm), `sma_incoming` -> SMA break + 15EMA retest, `compressed` -> Range breakout. An existing arm is never reset or duplicated; a failed or impossible arm is reported ("not armed (why)") and the veto still retires. All other reasons and ordinary claims/Focus membership stay manual-only.
- Pullback M15/M30 triggers (`pullback_sma_reclaim_v2`, 2026-09-23) fire only after a DIP (bar within 0.2 x daily Wilder ATR(20) of the SMA or through it, or a close break-and-reclaim) no older than 15 M30 / 30 M15 bars, then an LRSI cross DOWN through 20 at or after the dip began, then the 80 up-cross (M15 companion too, after the M30 flip). D1 ATR is from sessions before the cross day; missing D1 = the touch path is unknown, the close path still answers. `gate_blocked` annotates only.
- A day-trade PASS is a note, never a veto or chart retirement. Its codes have their own vocabulary (`pass_reasons_v*.json`); cached M5 bars are referenced through a sidecar written before the row. Capture never fetches bars, and a pass does not mark the symbol reviewed today.
- Alt+L is a QUICK like (`like_mode: quick`), never prompts; Alt+K is CLAIMED, and an absent mode reads as CLAIMED. A like grants no privilege and links to auto-tagging; it never writes a tag. Sidecar time reads attach the desk timezone to naive stamps and never strip an aware stamp.
- Every verdict (veto, like, pass, rejection) has a forward record; no two are combined. Rejection identities `focus__m5_not_today` and `focus__swing_dislike` are load-bearing. Like/veto/pass cohort merges share one click helper; failure adds a status suffix and never drops the nightly slot. Pass cohorts can overlap, and only `pass_all` counts passes. `unfavorite` is never graded. An unreachable grade is BLANK, never zero.
- Veto vocabulary is versioned and codes are never reused. Cohort identity is `(vocab_version, reason_code)`; rows are immutable and pooling occurs only in `_rebuild_pooled_performance`. Pre-version vetoes pool with the version that introduced their code. Never assert a literal `vocab_version` in a test.
- PROVEN is the top alert class. BANGER no longer exists; the `banger` column stays `False`.
- LRSI M5 alerts are retired at the EMIT seam only. Never flip `M5_SIGNAL_TYPE_DEFAULTS` for them; that would stop the evidence.
- Repetition folding and movers-only are presentation only. Repetition keeps one live symbol/side/day row, folds repeats with an ×N badge, lets privileged rows bypass the fold, and writes the backing list first. Movers-only hides and counts; neither filter withholds, deletes or writes policy. UNKNOWN always shows as `unmeasured` (or `at wall`, below).
- Wall gate (trader, 2026-09-23; `wall_gate.py`): a review chart within 1.0 x D1 Wilder ATR20 of an SMA 50/100/200 in its path (long: at/above price; short: at/below), within 1 ATR of the scan's D1 trendline on either side, or on the trendline's break day is hidden ONLY once its follow-up is armed: `sma_break` + `ema15_reject` for an SMA, an M15/M30 Pullback for a trendline (`auto_arm_watch`, never a take). Never duplicate a covering watch or re-arm a declined one (ledger `wall_gate_arms.json`, 10 sessions). Cap 20 live symbols; cap hit, failed arm, declined, or no cached M5 bars for a pullback -> it SHOWS as `at wall`. Missing data is UNKNOWN. Each hide writes `wall_hidden`. Kill switch `WALL_GATE_ENABLED`.
- M5 alerts are a list beside the chart. Moving from one row to another is a skip, never a requeue; clicking away IS a pass (`clicked_away_from_m5_alert`). Never "fix" that. With-trend rows auto-join M5 Focus in DESK only. `_is_m5_review_alert` routing belongs in `_enqueue_review_alert`, after the AWAY branch.
- "Holding highs" is measured in ATR (1.0), expires 15 minutes after the later of the alert and last new extreme, and is removed from the review queue only. Uncertainty never deletes.
- Hidden is not removed: the Alerts, D1 Focus and Armed tabs and the Universe page are hidden, but still built and indexed, and every rail hotkey still fires.
- Every ticker click charts into the centre pane through `chart_symbol`. A board chart holds no place in the waiting list.
- A chart opened from the setups table cycles through that table after veto, claim or Next. A day veto hides only veto/dislike/not_today/remove_today rows, counted by Show vetoed; a day-trade PASS and M5 click-away do not hide it. This is presentation only.

## Focus, gating and modes
- The M5 Focus adoption gate is defined once in `focus_adoption_gate.py`: beyond yesterday's extreme AND on the right side of `chart_snapshot.session_vwap_series`, on the last completed M5 bar. UNKNOWN fails. Stored verdicts expire after 45 minutes or 2 completed bars.
- A Focus pick's automatic D1 alerts are pullbacks only. Extensions fire only when the trader arms them; enforce this while generating flags, never by suppressing downstream alerts. The auto lane is exactly `D1_PULLBACK_KINDS` (15EMA reject, AVWAPE bounce, 1σ bounce); the grouped menu kinds (`d1_line_pullback`, `range_breakout`, `line_break`, `sma_break_retest`) are trader-armed only. A kind taken off the D1 menu keeps loading, firing and showing while armed so it can be disarmed.
- Armed alerts expire in TRADING days (5 for a five-day extreme watch, 10 otherwise), counted by `market_calendar.trading_days_between` in `armed_alert_expiry.py`. Uncertainty never deletes; every expiry appends a row, price alerts are disarmed never deleted, and expiry runs at the start of the poll owning that store.
- A quiet Focus pick fades reversibly after 10 trading days through `FocusPickStore`'s removal path; it never touches a watchlist line or writes a `pick_feedback` verdict. Keep the faded marker and append-only record; a faded swing favorite appends a retraction. Check on day roll and the half-hour timer.
- `focus_auto_picks.json` marks machine picks. No marker means the trader owns the pick; only marked entries may receive Not today or desync repair.
- Swing favourites: swing Focus is written first and must not fail, then `swing_favorites.jsonl` whose failure is swallowed. Never mark these as automatic picks; a removal appends a retraction. The strip stays below setups in the right D1 column; left is M5 only.
- Auto modes (trader, 2026-09-23): discovery is the same everywhere. DESK adopts. AWAY stages only and returns an EOD recap. EVENING scans like DESK all morning (plus the 07:00 early slot, strength checks and briefing), stages, never fills the review queue or M5 bar, empties the queue on the flip out and shows ONE catch-up card whose rows chart through the board door. OFF does nothing.
- Every automatic starter is gated on `autopilot_core.auto_scanning_due`, and fails open. Manual buttons are never gated.
- Phone push (trader, 2026-09-23): DESK sends NOTHING to the phone (desk sound, feed and queue unchanged; the manual test push stays). AWAY pushes routine output; price alerts and armed watches push once in AWAY/EVENING/OFF. In EVENING the SPY ±1% alarm latches and, like any price alert that fired, rings every 10 s until the mode changes: own timer, off the Qt thread, single-flight, no backoff, no cap; a failed send retries next tick.
- The adoption gate compares timestamps by attaching market-local time to the naive side. It never strips the aware side.

## Trade Mentor
- It prompts only a PRESENT trader: whole hours from 07:00 PT in DESK/EVENING/OFF, never in AWAY or as a push. The setting ships OFF. Each answer is one dated Market Journal row; missed hours are recorded skipped. This is the narrow fixed-time exception to quiet hours.
- The 09:00 slot checks all prior-session trades, and each trade is ASKED ONCE (trader 2026-09-23). Any answer opens that trade's Save; blank fields write no row. When the card is left (Submit, Read unchanged, Skip, next slot, hide), every trade still on it is filed as it stands and gets a `MENTOR_ASKED` marker; a marked trade is never offered again, except a new exit session, asked once. Local AI fills blanks off-Qt from the trader's own words (exact span, `filled_by: local_ai`) or leaves them blank; no follow-up. A failed journal write writes no marker. Completeness counts still count blanks; only the askable counts decide the ride. A waiting exit reading is shown once. Match answers by `trade_id`. A not-ready journal gets one Questrade retry. A `provisional` tag is not a confirmed setup; never offer or confirm a rejection tag as a setup. The setup guess lanes are claimed like, provisional tag, then the best stored setup-lane candidate (`evidence`), shown with its evidence; a guess nobody clicks writes nothing. Confirmed setup provenance uses the first fill's session, not `trade_date`.

- An observation (words) and a prediction (forced click) are separate; clicks-only text stays empty. A row's `timeframe` and prediction `horizon` always agree, enforced at the writer; Read unchanged reaffirms per timeframe and never falls back. One `trade_mentor_context_v2` builder serves the card and internals strip, marking missing inputs unmeasured. Save the 10:00 reply raw before any off-Qt draft fills missing fields; validate spans/units and keep existing values.
- A question kind ships only with a named consumer and answer key. A kind with no reader stays DORMANT: do not ask it or add a shim reader. At most 3 questions per card, excluding the forced trade section and prediction rows; count and carry excess questions. Verify consumers structurally, not with text search.
- One card starts at most one Questrade import. The three-day morning catch-up runs first when owed, outside the cap; otherwise `mentor_questions.pre_card_pull` is the sole owner of daytime Questrade attempts and the per-day cap. It runs on a reserved card, never in AWAY, never refreshes a token and is never awaited.
- An entry keeps its four fields; an exit is one box per trade and exit session; a same-session scale-out is one ask. Append-only `EXIT_NOTE_RAW` must be written before any read and fails loudly. The night drafts `why` from closed exit reasons, `felt` from its own vocabulary and `watching` as exact quotes, grounded in spans reproducing each quote. Give the drafter only the raw note, symbol, side and the two code lists. Existing values win; the draft is provisional until the trader confirms or corrects it, tied to `check.exit_key`. Rewriting is the only way to submit another note.

## Performance on the Qt thread
- Lists diff, never rebuild. Stylesheets count as expensive Qt work; widget variants live in `theme.qss`. Fonts are sized in px. Materialize chart bars through `ChartDataService.cached_bar_dicts`.
- A burst of one signal gets ONE reaction, coalesced at the LISTENER by `SignalCoalescer` (200 ms leading edge). Adoption drains at most 10 picks per cycle as pacing only; no pick is withheld or dropped, and a deferred pick is never marked seen.
- All cyclic GC runs on the GUI thread (`gc.disable()`). Activity may delay a sweep but never cancel it; every wait has a tick deadline. After the window shows, sweep the startup heap before `gc.freeze()` in `main()`.
- A candle that breaks `low <= open,close <= high` is drawn dashed and hollow, clamped, kept out of the scale, and logged; never silently dropped.
- `ScanCycleClock` measures and formats, decides nothing, and never calls `sleep`, `wait`, `start` or `Thread`.
- The exchange calendar is memoized; the stall watchdog's cap is per hour; `ui/thread_cpu_gauge.py` is always on and measures every thread once a minute. Never rate a timer from its docstring; measure it.
- The M5 scanner is one below-normal child process owned by `BounceService`; every GUI callback crosses the bounded queue, commands use the proxy, a dead child restarts on the health tick and shutdown retires it. Detector, timing and bars stay unchanged. Keep the duplicate fourth `_record_environment_focus_history` call: it is a scoring input and stays until asked.
- The pick scorecard runs on its own worker, reads today's rows only, and stamps `picks_scored_at` only on success; after 3 failures it gives up for the day with `picks_scoring_failed_at`.
