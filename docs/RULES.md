# Desk rules by area

Only read the section for the area you're touching. Each rule is binding. The incident
behind each one is in `notes/docs/DESK_INTERNALS.md` (local only); open the matching
entry there only if you plan to change the rule. If you change a rule, update this line
in the same commit.

## Shape and data
- One desk role. Desk Link/satellite, the mini-PC scanner, Tk UI, `TickerMover.py` and PyQt5 are REMOVED.
- Market data: IBKR `127.0.0.1:7496` primary, yfinance fallback, source tracked per scan. D1 daily bars are pinned to Yahoo by `local_settings.json` `daily_bars_source: "yahoo"` (that's intended, not an IB failure).
- `longs.txt`/`shorts.txt` are the day-trade lists, wiped whole after each session's close by `scripts/daytrade_watchlist_reset.py` (decision 0020). Each name gets one `session_reset` remove row. Swing lists are never touched.
- `C:\TradingBotData` is a plain local folder, not a cloud drive. Machine caches live under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TradingBotV3`. The DAS is the durable tier: write locally first, move to the DAS after.
- Shadow engines (`market_state.py`, `greatness_monitor`) only emit JSONL evidence.

## Research warehouse (shadow only, zero detector/score/alert influence)
- Lives at `research_store_dir`, never inside `C:\TradingBotData`. Unset means disabled.
- The post-scan build runs in a below-normal CHILD PROCESS, never a thread. Reads are month-keyed through `ResearchStore.read_rows`.
- Never widen `_run_outcomes` to a date filter. It walks forward across sessions.
- Seal dedupes at the dataset grain. Repair with `research_warehouse.cli dedupe --apply` (it's a dry run by default).
- A snapshot over 64 MB is never `json.loads`-ed. The M5 tee dedupes before any per-bar work, using `tee_high_water.json`.
- H2/H4 exist for the HTF LRSI study only. Live `CROSS_LEVELS` stays `(20, 50)`.
- `earnings_avwap_anchors.csv` is append-only and fed by the scan. Never trim it. A reconstructed anchor is labelled and never counts as promotion evidence.
- The daily snapshot keeps `avwape_*` (champion) and `avwap_variant_*` (challenger) in separate columns, always.
- The setup registry is frozen data regenerated by `build_setup_registry.py --write`. An unknown name raises.
- `setup_research` narration is a bounded view selected by size (`stats.n`), never by an R statistic.

## Alert Center and review
- The arm bar stays UNDER the chart. Never propose moving it without asking. Rail shortcuts are bound at panel scope.
- A VETO retires the chart. A CLAIMED like advances it. A QUICK like (Alt+L) and a NOTE move nothing. Both likes write `like_advance`.
- A claimed like on a D1 chart saves to `claimed_picks.jsonl` first, then retires. An active claim hides that D1 scan alert from the REVIEW QUEUE only.
- A day-trade PASS is a note, not a veto. Its codes are their own vocabulary (`pass_reasons_v*.json`), and bars are referenced through a sidecar written before the row.
- Every verdict (veto, like, pass, rejection) has a forward record, and no two are combined. `unfavorite` is never graded. An unreachable grade is BLANK, never zero.
- Veto vocabulary is versioned and codes are never reused. Never assert a literal `vocab_version` in a test.
- PROVEN is the top alert class. BANGER no longer exists; the `banger` column stays `False`.
- LRSI M5 alerts are retired at the EMIT seam only. Never flip `M5_SIGNAL_TYPE_DEFAULTS` for them; that would stop the evidence.
- Repetition folding and movers-only are presentation only. They never withhold, delete or write policy. UNKNOWN always shows.
- M5 alerts are a list beside the chart. Clicking away IS a pass (`clicked_away_from_m5_alert`). Never "fix" that.
- "Holding highs" is measured in ATR (1.0), expires 15 minutes after the last extreme, and uncertainty never deletes.
- Every ticker click charts into the centre pane through `chart_symbol`. A board chart holds no place in the waiting list.
- A chart opened from the setups table cycles through that table. A day veto HIDES the row (presentation only).

## Focus, gating and modes
- The M5 Focus adoption gate is defined once, in `focus_adoption_gate.py`: beyond yesterday's extreme AND on the right side of session VWAP, on the last completed bar. UNKNOWN fails.
- A Focus pick's automatic D1 alerts are pullbacks only. Extensions fire only when the trader arms them.
- Armed alerts expire in TRADING days (5 or 10) via `armed_alert_expiry.py`. They're disarmed, never deleted.
- A quiet Focus pick fades after 10 trading days, reversibly, and never touches a watchlist line.
- `focus_auto_picks.json` marks machine picks. No marker means the trader owns the pick.
- Swing favourites: swing Focus is written first (must not fail), then `swing_favorites.jsonl` (its failure is swallowed).
- Auto modes: discovery is the same everywhere. DESK adopts, AWAY stages only, EVENING runs the early slot and then stops, OFF does nothing.
- Every automatic starter is gated on `autopilot_core.auto_scanning_due`, and fails open. Manual buttons are never gated.
- Phone push: only AWAY pushes routine output. The exceptions are price alerts (every mode) and EVENING's SPY ±1% alarm.
- The adoption gate compares timestamps by attaching market-local time to the naive side. It never strips the aware side.

## Trade Mentor
- It prompts only a PRESENT trader: whole hours from 07:00 PT in DESK/EVENING/OFF, never in AWAY, never as a push. The setting ships OFF.
- The 09:00 slot is the forced trade check. Saving is PER TRADE: an answered trade is stored at once and never asked again.
- A `provisional` tag is not an answer. A rejection tag is never offered as a setup.
- An observation (words) and a prediction (a forced click) are separate. A row's `timeframe` and its prediction's `horizon` always agree, enforced at the writer.
- A question kind ships only with a named consumer. A kind with no reader stays DORMANT. At most 3 questions per card.
- One card starts at most one Questrade import (`mentor_questions.pre_card_pull`).
- An exit is one box (`EXIT_NOTE_RAW`, written before anything reads it). The night drafts why/felt/watching blind to the outcome. The draft is provisional until the trader clicks.

## Performance on the Qt thread
- Lists diff, never rebuild. Widget variants live in `theme.qss`. Fonts are sized in px.
- A burst of one signal gets ONE reaction (`SignalCoalescer`, 200 ms). Adoption drains 10 picks per cycle and never drops one.
- All cyclic GC runs on the GUI thread (`gc.disable()`). Startup heap is swept, then `gc.freeze()`.
- A candle that breaks `low <= open,close <= high` is drawn dashed and logged, never dropped.
- `ScanCycleClock` measures only (no sleep, wait, start or Thread).
- The M5 scanner is ONE below-normal child process owned by `BounceService`.
- The pick scorecard runs on its own worker and gives up after 3 failures a day.

## Evidence, journal and statistics
- Human-focus pick key: `(trade_date, symbol, side, category slot)`.
- Evidence stores never cost the thing they record. The one exception: a journal WRITE fails loudly.
- Statistics live once in `scripts/evidence_stats.py`. `outcome_semantics` decides what can be averaged.
- `unresolved` means UNMEASURED. Every reader goes through `outcome_semantics.terminal_kind`.
- Market Journal (what the trader thought) and Journal (what they traded) are two stores, never merged, both reached via `shared_journal_service()`.
- Day Review reads ONE payload on ONE worker. No machine row appears on it (`is_machine_entry`).
- The night narrates, never grades. Output that disagrees with its pack is rejected WHOLE. Bounds come from the pack.
- The report card and Week Review compute nothing of their own. They say what they couldn't see and never print zero for unknown.
- AI ideas are suggestions. Nothing they write reaches a detector, score, alert, watchlist, Focus, the queue or `review_policy.json`.
- Mood is reported only. Nothing that acts may read it.
- Real miss = `REAL_MISS_V1` (1.0 ATR in favour before 0.5 ATR against). D1 decisions are measured on daily bars. A pooled rate counts a name only once its horizon has closed.
- `session_date` is unchanged. A decision belongs to the session it JUDGED (`decision_session`, an additive field).
- A clicked read is never pooled with an inferred one (`PoolingError`). Pending is never superseded by unmeasured.
- Auto-tagging has four lanes, ordered by lane, never by confidence. No tag is ever derived from the outcome.
- The trader owns `trade_annotations`. The one machine writer (`journal_bulk_tag.py`) writes only `provisional` tags.
- A broker file is authoritative for money, not time: fills sit at midnight, commission keeps its SIGN (never `abs()`), and the file overrides the live sync on money via `VOID_EXECUTION` rows.
- Questrade: `STO` means SELL. Stored rows move only through `journal_reclassify.py`, which is the trader's act.
- The tax number is the broker's (`raw_executions.net_amount`). It refuses rather than estimates.
- The Questrade token refresh has ONE owner (`refresh_access_token` under the lock). Repair is a trader action.
- The tracker JSON is the truth. The SQLite mirror is shadow until gate #57.
- Tracker replay defaults are `gap_aware_v2` / `prior_session_v2` / `first_actionable_v2`, and every record is stamped.
- The overnight stage order (decision 0018) is: deterministic, digest, narration, model-gated. It's pinned in `EXPECTED_SLOT_ORDER`.
- Local inference runs at night only, seven days a week. `--force` never buys the clock for a model slot.
- The digest gate needs both a clean window AND `approve-audit` (a trader command).

## Charts and boards
- D1 charts: the volume underlay and earnings ribbon never vote on the price range. Levels are built off the paint path.
- The group RS/RW tape and the M5 Strength Board each use ONE batched yfinance call per tick, with zero IB traffic.
- The Strength window is one flat scrolling page.
- One completed-bar rule (`completed_bars.py`): `bar_start + bar_minutes <= now`, compared with `astimezone`.
- Pure indicators (`scripts/indicators/`) take completed bars in, return immutable tuples, and use `None` for unmeasurable.
- A note marker sits on a bar only if it happened during that bar. A marker past the end of the tape is counted and never clamped.

## Headline statistics (decision 0016)
- Swing surfaces lead with win rate, plus `n` and the Wilson lower bound (z 1.96, one Wilson everywhere).
- Day-trade surfaces lead with `held_run_score` = P(held in the first 30 min) × trimmed-mean MFE_R. Held means MEASURED held.
- "Lately" = `evidence_stats.LATELY_SESSIONS` (20 sessions). Every surface says "sessions", never "days".
- The priority switch (`prioritise_working_lately`, default OFF) reorders and never withholds.
- Research isn't a trader surface, except the Results page.
